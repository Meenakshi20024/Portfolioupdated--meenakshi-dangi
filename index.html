about me

Code. Design. Strategy. All in one.

I’m a Full Stack Developer from Bhopal with a BCA (Hons.) in Full Stack Development from Jagran Lakecity University, now pursuing an MBA at Makhanlal Chaturvedi National University.

I build clean, responsive web apps with the MERN stack and design them with UI/UX in mind. My MBA adds the business side: understanding users, reading data and turning ideas into solutions that matter. Beyond coding, I’ve been a hackathon finalist, a national conference presenter, a published researcher, and a core member of my university’s design and editorial team.

My goal: to combine tech and business as a Business Analyst or Full Stack Developer, building products that create real impact.
